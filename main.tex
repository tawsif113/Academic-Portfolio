learning, and trustworthy evaluation. Current work develops a reproducible
privacy--utility audit for machine-learning-based network intrusion detection,

\section*{Research Direction for PhD Study}

I am interested in practical security and machine-learning systems in which model
utility, privacy leakage, and formal protection are evaluated under one explicit,
reproducible protocol. My current study asks whether formally accounted DP-SGD can
reduce measurable training-membership leakage in a tabular intrusion detector
while retaining operationally acceptable Recall and false-negative rate. Long
term, I want to develop deployable trustworthy learning systems whose privacy and
reliability claims remain aligned with empirical evidence.

\section*{Research Experience}

\project{A Reproducible Privacy--Utility Audit for ML-Based Network Intrusion Detection}{2026 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Designed a locked NSL-KDD binary-classification protocol with
    validation-only threshold selection and an untouched KDDTest+ evaluation
    split.
    \item Established Random Forest, XGBoost, and MLP utility baselines; the
    selected non-private MLP achieved 76.91\% Recall, 23.09\% FNR, and 84.08\%
    F1 on KDDTest+ at a validation-selected threshold of 0.28.
    \item Completed a five-shadow-model membership-inference audit with
    score-only and label-aware features. The strongest overall baseline attack
    was near chance (ROC-AUC approximately 0.5029), establishing an important
    floor-effect limitation for later privacy comparisons.
    \item Completed PyTorch parity and an Opacus DP-SGD feasibility run with
    explicit accounting at $\varepsilon=7.9986$ and
    $\delta=1.134\times10^{-5}$. The single smoke test achieved 66.20\% Recall,
    33.80\% FNR, 76.80\% F1, and 91.93\% PR-AUC.
    \item Current work: execute the full privacy-budget sweep, apply the same
    membership audit to every DP model, and estimate uncertainty across
    repeated runs. The feasibility result is not yet evidence of reduced
    membership leakage.
    \item Repository:
    \href{https://github.com/tawsif113/privacy-utility-dp-ids}{github.com/tawsif113/privacy-utility-dp-ids}.
\end{itemize}
