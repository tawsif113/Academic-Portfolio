\section*{Research Direction for PhD Study}

I am interested in building practical and reproducible security/ML systems that evaluate the tradeoff between model utility, privacy leakage, and robustness. A near-term direction is to reproduce and extend privacy-preserving ML methods for network intrusion detection using public benchmark datasets, then evaluate both predictive performance and attack-aware metrics such as membership inference risk. Long term, I want to work on deployable trustworthy AI systems where privacy, robustness, and reliability are measured together rather than treated as separate goals.
